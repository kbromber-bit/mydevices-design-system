One-line: the brand's honest placeholder — it states what asset is missing instead of faking one.

```jsx
<Placeholder label="Provide asset" note={<>headshot or plant photo<br />portrait ≈900×1120</>} ratio="4/5" />
<Placeholder shape="avatar" size={44} />
```

Pair placeholders with a `SourceNote` telling the reader they get replaced. Never substitute stock imagery or an invented logo.
