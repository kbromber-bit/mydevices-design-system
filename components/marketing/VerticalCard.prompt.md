One-line: the use-case grid card — vertical name, one plain sentence, and an outcome link.

```jsx
<VerticalCard rule="var(--md-cat-machine)" title="Machine health & OEE" linkLabel="See failures caught early">
  Vibration, runtime, and power drawn from equipment, flagged before it fails.
</VerticalCard>
```

Link copy always names the OUTCOME, not the page: "See excursions flagged →", never "Learn more".
