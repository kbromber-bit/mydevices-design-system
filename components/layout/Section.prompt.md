One-line: wraps every band of the page in the brand's standard 96px/28px rhythm and one of five sanctioned backgrounds.

```jsx
<Section tone="wash-hero" width="narrow" align="center">…hero…</Section>
<Section tone="alt">…proof…</Section>
<Section tone="navy" width="narrow" align="center">…stats…</Section>
```

Only ONE `navy` section per page. Alternate `light` and `alt` for everything else — never a third background colour.
