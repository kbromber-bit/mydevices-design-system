One-line: one turn of the in-AI conversation — use it inside `ChatPanel`.

```jsx
<ChatMessage>Which equipment are our significant energy users this month?</ChatMessage>
<ChatMessage role="assistant" avatar="../../assets/ai/claude.png"
  status="68 devices ranked · SEU flags applied at 5% of load" statusDone
  insight="Cure & shrink ovens are 31.7% of submetered load — the top system group."
  confirm="The SEU register, updated live. Sort, filter, or drop it into your EnMS.">
  <ArtifactCard … />
</ChatMessage>
```

Insights always carry a specific figure. Never write "AI-powered" anything in the copy.
